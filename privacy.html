Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

About This Project

Pokémon Card Alerts is a personal project that uses software to track Pokémon card prices and send notifications to a designated mobile phone number.

Information Processed

The project may process a mobile phone number, Pokémon card inventory information, price data, alert settings, and message delivery information needed to operate the notification system.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to generate price alerts, valuation updates, inventory summaries, and system notifications.

Sharing and Service Providers

Personal information is not sold. Service providers, including messaging providers, may process information as necessary to operate the service and deliver messages.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Data Retention and Security

Information is retained as needed to operate and maintain this project. Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information, although no method of storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project operator through the contact method provided by the operator.
