Program Description

Pokémon Card Alerts is a personal project that generates informational notifications about Pokémon card prices, valuation changes, inventory summaries, and system status.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on price changes, inventory updates, and the operation of the alert script. Approximately 100 messages per month are anticipated, but the actual number may vary.

Message and data rates may apply depending on your wireless carrier and mobile plan.
